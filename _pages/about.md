I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the **School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University**. My research focuses on **AI for Biology**, with particular interests in mathematical and generative modeling of **single-cell dynamics**, **gene regulatory systems**, and **cellular perturbation responses**.

My work lies at the interface of **applied mathematics**, **stochastic modeling**, and **machine learning**. A central theme of my research is to develop principled computational methods for learning biological dynamics from high-dimensional and often incomplete observations, especially single-cell snapshot data. I am particularly interested in combining mathematical structure and biological prior knowledge with modern generative models to obtain models that are both predictive and mechanistically meaningful.

On the methodological side, my recent work focuses on **flow matching**, **optimal transport**, and related generative modeling frameworks for learning continuous and discrete biological dynamics. In particular, I study how transport geometry, population growth and death, discrete molecular states, and biologically informed conditional paths can be incorporated into dynamical models of cell populations.

Another major direction of my research concerns **stochastic biochemical reaction networks** and the **chemical master equation**. I am interested in connecting mechanistic stochastic models with neural likelihood and generative approaches for efficient distribution learning, parameter inference, and prediction of gene expression dynamics.

More broadly, my goal is to develop mathematical and AI-based frameworks that connect **forward dynamical modeling**, **mechanistic inference**, and **perturbation design**, with applications to single-cell biology and the development of predictive **AI virtual cell** models.

My current research topics include:

- **AI for Biology** and mathematical modeling of single-cell dynamics
- **Flow matching** and generative modeling for biological dynamical systems
- **Optimal transport** and unbalanced transport for cell-state and population dynamics
- **Discrete and count-space generative models** for gene expression dynamics
- **Stochastic gene regulatory networks** and biochemical reaction systems
- **Neural likelihood and parameter inference** for chemical master equation models
- **Single-cell perturbation prediction**, cell-fate control, and intervention design
- **AI virtual cell** and predictive modeling of cellular dynamics
